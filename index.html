rclone: Personal Google Drive Sync

A privately configured Google OAuth client for synchronizing the owner's Google Drive accounts with local folders.

What this application does

The owner runs rclone on their own computer to download Drive files and upload local files. Scheduled two-way synchronization also propagates edits and deletions.

Google Drive authorization lets the local application read file contents and metadata, create and update files, and move or delete files as required by the owner's sync configuration.

This client is intended only for the owner's authorized accounts. It is not a public sync service, and this site is not the official rclone website or rclone's shared OAuth application.

Where your data goes

Synchronization takes place between Google Drive and the owner's computer. This static website does not receive Drive files, OAuth tokens, or account credentials.

For details about data access, storage, and revoking access, see the privacy policy.

Contact
